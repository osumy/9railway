9Railway

Connect your Railway account to manage your 9Router services.
Why do I need to sign in?

9Railway works with your own Railway account — it deploys and manages your 9Router services using your project space.

Signing in lets the tool create projects and services on your behalf, so everything (API keys, service URLs) is stored on your side.

You'll be taken to Railway's official sign-in page in a new tab. We never see your Railway password. When you approve, we only receive a scoped token that can manage your own projects — nothing else.
